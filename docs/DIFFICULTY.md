2. *(multiply, tier 1)* 37 x 10 = ? — 47, 407, **370**, 380
3. *(homophones, tier 1)* I ___ the answer correctly. Which spelling is right here? — capitol, capital, **guessed**, guest
4. *(synonyms, tier 1)* Which word is a synonym of intelligent? — weird, **smart**, swift, dirty
5. *(time, tier 2)* Nina's game starts at 8:40 and lasts 25 minutes. When does it end? — **9:05**, 9:15, 8:55, 10:05
6. *(wordproblems, tier 2)* A hall has 7 rows of 18 chairs. 57 chairs are empty. How many chairs are being used? — **69**, 126, 183, 82
7. *(compounds, tier 2)* Over + pass = overpass News + print = newsprint Stock + room = stockroom Night + fall = ______. — **nightfall**, nighttime, downfall, windfall
8. *(plurals, tier 2)* Today I leave early. Yesterday I ___ early. — lefted, **left**, leaft, leaved
9. *(vocabulary, tier 3)* "deteriorate" means ___. — to stay the same, **to get worse**, to disappear, to improve
10. *(time, tier 3)* How much time passes from 3:45 to 4:55? — 1 hour, **1 hour 10 minutes**, 1 hour 15 minutes, 2 hours 10 minutes
11. *(multiply, tier 3)* 7 x 35 = ? — 255, **245**, 235, 42
12. *(fractions, tier 3)* 1/5 + 1/5 = ? — 1/2, 3/5, **2/5**, 3/10

### 5th grade

1. *(synonyms, tier 1)* Which word means almost the same as method? — residence, regulation, argument, **procedure**
2. *(evenodd, tier 1)* Which number is composite? — 23, 31, 17, **24**
3. *(sequences, tier 1)* 4, 9, 16, 25, ... What comes next? — 37, 34, 38, **36**
4. *(time, tier 1)* It is 9:00 a.m. What time will it be in 4 hours 15 minutes? — **1:15 p.m.**, 2:15 p.m., 1:15 a.m., 1:20 p.m.
5. *(vocabulary, tier 2)* Which word means "unclear in meaning"? — resilient, exemplary, **ambiguous**, plausible
6. *(analogies, tier 2)* reluctant : unwilling Which pair goes together in the same way? — dog : puppy, pilot : plane, **furious : enraged**, hen : chick
7. *(homophones, tier 2)* The nurse found a ___ in my arm. Which spelling is right here? — idol, vane, vain, **vein**
8. *(compare, tier 2)* Which list is in order from greatest to least? — 0.961, 0.358, 0.803, 0.803, 0.961, 0.358, 0.358, 0.803, 0.961, **0.961, 0.803, 0.358**
9. *(fractions, tier 3)* 5 4/5 + 2 1/2 = ? — **8 3/10**, 9 3/10, 8 1/5, 8 2/5
10. *(homophones, tier 3)* The ___ up the mountain took all day. Which word fills the blank? — **ascent**, assent, burrow, hanger
11. *(associations, tier 3)* Which one is used for controlling temperature? — calendar, **thermostat**, barometer, kiln
12. *(measurement, tier 3)* 4.25 kilometers = ? meters — 42,500 meters, 5,250 meters, **4,250 meters**, 425 meters
